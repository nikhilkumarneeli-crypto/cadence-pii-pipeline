3. Structural Components Evaluated (10 Dimensions)

  • Slide Preservation (15%): Validates that all slide boundaries are preserved without omissions.
  • Title Preservation (10%): Detects slide titles, section headers, and semantic topic anchors.
  • Text Block Preservation (15%): Extracts discrete body text boxes and auto-shapes.
  • Paragraph Preservation (10%): Maintains bullet points and paragraph boundaries within shapes.
  • Table Preservation (10%): Accurately identifies 2D tabular structures on slides.
  • Table Cell Preservation (15%): Extracts every cell with row, column, header, and coordinate tracking.
  • Chart Text Preservation (5%): Pulls chart titles, series names, categories, and axis labels.
  • Speaker Notes Preservation (5%): Isolates confidential presenter notes associated with each slide.
  • Reading-Order Preservation (10%): Enforces natural 2D top-down, left-to-right reading sequence via Kendall's tau rank correlation.
  • OCR Coverage (5%): Detects embedded graphics and routes through pluggable OCR adapters.
